## About the blog

The texts published here follow their own editorial rules:

- every claim that comes from another source has at least one reference;
- references follow the Brazilian ABNT standard unless the article states another style;
- every article has an abstract and a plain-language version;
- charts and images have a text description and, when there is data, a table.
