## What this site collects

Nothing. This site uses no cookies, has no visitor analytics, has no forms and stores no information about you.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, as described in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## External content

Some articles may include videos or other third-party content. They only load if you press the load button. From that moment, the external service receives a request from your browser and its own privacy policy applies.
